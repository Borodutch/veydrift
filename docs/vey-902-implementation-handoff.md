## Boundaries and measured gas

- One typed atomic transaction per body; 15 rows count orders, not unit quantity. Planet Game embeds a new `VeydriftBatchTransportModule` address in its constructor; MoonSystem links a new `VeydriftMoonProductionBatch` library. The 16-entry **per-lane backlog cap remains unchanged**; active production is separate. A fresh body accepts 15 rows; preexisting backlog can reduce available slots. Child settlement occurs before the resulting-backlog check and a failing child rolls back the entire transaction.
- Foundry pinned `0.8.28`, optimizer 1, via IR, no CBOR metadata. Fresh planet heterogeneous 15-row success: 3,052,281 execution gas. Fresh moon 15-row success with fighters/rockets/dome and variable quantity: 2,999,547 execution gas. Planet and moon last-row overspend roll back atomically; 16 rows reject. Per-lane backlog 16 rejects overflow without changing paid queue state.
- Fresh **15 defense-only eligible rows** succeed with active+14 FIFO pending, checked ordered IDs/quantities/ready times and resource deductions: planet 3,221,550 execution / 3,267,190 conservative transaction bound; moon 2,581,199 / 2,626,839. At active+14, a third defense row overflows after two otherwise eligible rows; the call rolls back complete queue/resources/reserves, then the two-row prefix succeeds through active+16 pending.
- **Combined loaded settlement and costly heterogeneous proof:** 17 preexisting 100-unit orders per lane (one active + **16 pending** in each of ship and defense), distributed over seven ship and six defense types; all due and settled in the same call as 15 new varied costly ship/defense orders, including both unique dome guards. Planet 5,486,773 execution / 5,532,413 conservative transaction bound; moon 4,870,168 / 4,915,808. Prior cheap repeated-order fixture had 15+15 backlogs, not the full pending boundary; these strengthened fixtures replace that narrower claim. All are eligible actual success with settled inventories and new queues asserted. The upper bound uses 21,000 + 16 gas for **every** encoded calldata byte, deliberately overcounting zero bytes. Both remain below the unchanged 12,000,000 wallet preflight guard and Base's documented 16,777,216 per-transaction maximum (https://docs.base.org/specifications/transactions/throughput-and-limits).
- These are local eligibility/state probes, **not** keyless simulation against freshly read live state. The parent must perform that release gate before broadcasting or merging the frontend.
