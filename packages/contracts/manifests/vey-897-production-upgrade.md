## Limits and ABI

`startProductionBatch(uint256,(uint8 kind,uint8 itemId,uint32 quantity)[])` targets Game; `startMoonProductionBatch(...)` targets Moon. `kind=0` ship, `kind=1` defense. Each batch contains 1–4 orders, cumulative on-chain resource debit and full-transaction rollback on any child error. Batch lane backlog cap is 16 **remaining after each child settles ready entries**; single-production moon ship queue also caps at 16; existing planet and moon defense singles remain unchanged. Moon manufacturing excludes SolarSatellite, Pathfinder, Crawler and moon missiles. Game settles due moon ships before body-aware moon-origin fleet/ACS validation, and by historical cutoff before attack snapshots. A later Attack cannot settle before an earlier hostile arrival when Moon manufacturing would be credited; the lazy due-arrival prepass preserves earlier Deploy/Transport before Moon combat. Moon finish/owner-admin paths cannot mint post-impact ships while combat remains unresolved. The frontend revalidates fresh per-body costs/eligibility and exact calldata using pending/latest `eth_call`, then bounds measured `eth_estimateGas` at 12m; on-chain rules are final.
